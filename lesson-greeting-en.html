Make a thank-you picture with ChatGPT

Make a thank-you picture and prepare a spoken thank-you for your avatar.

  1. Compare two styles

    Compare these prepared styles. Choose one you like, then generate just one main picture.

  2. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    Create a square thank-you picture: an original orange cat with paws together, cream background and warm illustrated style. Include only the large clear words “Thank you”. No other text or existing brands.

  3. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  4. Check your thank-you picture

    Check spelling and whether you like the picture.

  5. Open the generated image

    Tap the outlined picture.

  6. Tap Download to save

    Tap the circled download arrow.

  7. Draft a correction

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Do not generate another image. Write one clear correction request: keep the picture and enlarge the text so it is easier to read on a phone.

  8. Prepare a spoken thank-you

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Prepare a friendly thank-you line lasting about ten seconds: Thank you for carrying my shopping bags. Let’s have tea together next time! Do not add details.

  9. Keep your thank-you line

    Copy this line into your phone notes for Flow.

    Thank you for carrying my shopping bags. Let’s have tea together next time!

  10. Keep your avatar prompt

    Copy this prompt into your phone notes to use directly in the Flow app.

    @me Create a 10-second 9:16 video on a simple background. Say naturally and clearly: “Thank you for carrying my shopping bags. Let’s have tea together next time!” with one gentle wave. Keep my likeness and voice. Add no other people, dialogue or captions.

  11. Keep the video prompt for later

    Copy this video prompt into your notes too. Use it directly in Flow without returning to ChatGPT.

    Animate this cat with one gentle nod and blink. Keep the original style, background and full picture. Fixed camera; no zoom or cropping. No added text, people or objects. No dialogue.

Three more things to try

  • Invite a friend for tea

    Make a picture inviting a friend for tea.

  • Send birthday wishes

    Make a birthday picture for someone in your family.

  • Wish someone a lovely weekend

    Make an animal illustration that says “Have a lovely weekend”.
