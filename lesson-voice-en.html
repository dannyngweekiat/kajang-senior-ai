Ask with your voice

Speak a shop-hours question and get Chinese and Malay wording.

  1. Tap the microphone

    In a quiet place, tap the app or keyboard microphone. Choose the microphone permission yourself if requested.

  2. Try asking this

    Hold the phone nearby and say this slowly.

    Prepare a short, polite question asking what time the shop closes today. Give it in Chinese and Malay.

  3. Check the words, then send

    Check names, numbers and places, then tap the send arrow.

  4. Try another wording

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Make both versions shorter and suitable for asking in person.

  5. Check the reply

    Check both versions ask about closing time. Confirm unfamiliar Malay wording with someone who speaks it.

Five more things to try

  • Message your family

    Say what you want to tell your family and ask ChatGPT to turn it into a message.

  • Find a meal idea

    Name the ingredients in your fridge and ask what you could cook.

  • Try a translation

    Say a sentence in Mandarin and ask for a Malay translation.

  • Dictate a shopping list

    Say what you need to buy and the quantities, ask for a list and check it.

  • Arrange a meeting

    Dictate a message arranging to meet a friend, checking the date, time and place before sending.
