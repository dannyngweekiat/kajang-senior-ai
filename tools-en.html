How do we plan free usage?

Plan one image generation and one photo upload today. These are classroom targets, not guaranteed free allowances. Also plan one video in Google Flow and check its credit cost first.

Flow currently provides 50 daily credits without a subscription; unused credits do not carry over. Costs depend on the model and output count. Check the cost shown before generating.

Plan one video and choose x1. If credits or generation are unavailable, follow the screenshots in order and prepare your picture and prompt for later. No credit purchase is needed.

At a limit, pause retries and follow the displayed reset time. Use the lesson example to prepare your wording and prompt. Continue text practice in a separate text-only chat. No subscription, account sharing is needed.
