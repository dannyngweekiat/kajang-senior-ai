Before the workshop

Bring your phone and these details to get started. We will demonstrate each activity, and you can return to the guide after class.

  1. Phone and charger

    Charge your phone and bring the cable and your usual glasses. Bring a power bank if you have one.

  2. Your own account

    Have your own ChatGPT and Google accounts ready. Use your Google account for Flow. Enter passwords and verification codes yourself and keep them private.

  3. Your photos

    Bring one menu or object photo without private details. Sign in beforehand and try to reserve image and upload allowance for class. The thank-you picture needs no photo upload.

  4. Connect to Wi-Fi

    Connect to reliable Wi-Fi or mobile data. Install the official Flow app from Tools for avatar lessons; use flow.google.com in your phone browser for cat and product videos. Complete your own face and voice capture privately, outside public class recordings.

  5. Switch between tabs

    Tools open in a new browser tab. To come back, tap your browser’s tabs button and choose this guide. We will demonstrate how to switch during class.
