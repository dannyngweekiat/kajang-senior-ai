Use AI safely

Keep personal information private and double-check important details.

  1. Keep private details safe

    Keep passwords, verification codes, IDs, banking details and medical records out of ChatGPT.

  2. Read this fictional message

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Fictional message: “Your parcel is delayed. Pay RM2 immediately through this unfamiliar link or it will be returned.” Identify warning signs and explain how to verify independently through official channels. Do not open the link.

  3. Check the claims

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    Does a familiar profile picture or voice make a message trustworthy? Give two independent ways to verify it.

  4. Check important information

    Verify prices, dates and advice with reliable sources. Voices and photos can be fake; contact people using a trusted number.

  5. Verify before using

    Keep passwords, codes, identity and bank details private. Contact people with a trusted number rather than one supplied in the suspicious message.

Five more things to try

  • Check a prize message

    Ask ChatGPT for common signs of a suspicious prize message.

  • Keep personal details private

    Use a fictional example to ask which personal details should stay private.

  • Verify a claim

    Ask how to check a claim, then verify it with trusted sources.

  • Check a request for money

    Practise with a fictional urgent request for money from a friend and decide how to verify it using a trusted number.

  • Check a picture’s source

    Use a fictional example to ask how to verify a forwarded picture, then look for reliable sources yourself.
