Ask a question and follow up

Start with gardening, a hobby or cooking, then add a condition.

  1. Open ChatGPT

    Sign in with your own account and find the outlined message box.

  2. Try asking this

    Copy this text, then paste it into ChatGPT’s message box.

    I enjoy gardening. Suggest three simple gardening activities to try at home, using short sentences. Ask one question at a time if you need more details.

  3. Send your question

    Follow the marks: press and hold the message box to paste, then tap the send arrow.

  4. Add a condition

    Copy this text and send it in the same ChatGPT chat.

    I live in an apartment with only a balcony. Adapt the suggestions for a balcony and give simple steps.

  5. Read and refine

    Check the advice fits your space. Try cooking or a familiar hobby and ask one follow-up question.

Five more things to try

  • Find an activity

    Suggest three things to do at home on a rainy day.

  • Explore local food

    Tell me about Kajang satay in simple words.

  • Invite a friend for tea

    Write a short message inviting a friend for tea.

  • Use what you have

    Name three ingredients and ask ChatGPT for a meal idea, then add which cooking equipment you have.

  • Explore a hobby

    Choose a hobby you enjoy, ask about one aspect of it, then ask for an example.
