Bring your cat picture to life with Google Flow

Make your cat nod and blink in a fun short video you can save and share.

  1. Choose your cat picture

    Use your saved thank-you picture, or tap Save example picture to save this one to your phone.

  2. Open Google Flow

    Open flow.google.com in your phone browser and sign in with your Google account. This lesson uses the website.

  3. Start a new project

    Tap the highlighted New project button.

  4. Open the video settings

    Tap the highlighted settings beside the prompt box.

  5. Choose Video

    Tap the highlighted Video option.

  6. Choose Frames

    Tap Frames to use a picture as the first frame.

  7. Choose a vertical video

    Tap the highlighted 9:16 option.

  8. Choose one output

    Choose Veo 3.1 - Lite and x1 for one video.

  9. Add your first frame

    Tap outside the settings to close them, then tap Start. Leave End empty.

  10. Choose a picture from your phone

    Tap the circled upload arrow to open your phone’s photo picker.

  11. Select your picture

    Tap the cat picture you saved.

  12. Confirm the photo

    After choosing one picture, tap Add (1).

  13. First-upload notice

    If this notice appears, read it first. Tap I agree only after confirming you have the right to use the picture and agree to the notice.

  14. Add the uploaded picture

    Wait for the upload to finish, then tap the picture to add it as the first frame.

  15. Check your selected picture

    Check that the first-frame thumbnail shows the picture you chose.

  16. Describe the movement

    Copy this text. Return to your existing Flow tab, press and hold the prompt box, then tap Paste.

    Animate this cat with one gentle nod and blink. Keep the original style, background and full picture. Fixed camera; no zoom or cropping. No added text, people or objects. No dialogue.

  17. Check the credit cost

    Open settings and check the cost for this generation. Confirm one output and that your balance covers it.

  18. Generate once and wait

    Tap outside settings to close them, then tap the circled generation arrow once. Wait for the clip to appear.

  19. Play and check

    Open the generated clip and tap Play. Check the cat’s movement and whether anything you wanted to keep was cropped.

  20. Open the download menu

    Tap the top-right three dots, then Download media.

  21. Save the original video

    Tap Original size to save the video.

  22. Share a little smile

    Find the clip in browser downloads or Files. Share it with family or friends with “A little AI cat video to make you smile.”

Five more things to try

  • Make the cat wave

    Make the same cat gently wave, keeping the camera still.

  • Animate some flowers

    Make the flowers in a picture gently sway in the breeze.

  • Make the clouds drift

    Make the clouds in a landscape picture drift slowly.

  • Make a dog wag its tail

    Choose a dog picture and request a gentle tail wag, keeping its appearance and background unchanged.

  • Animate a butterfly

    Choose a butterfly picture and request gently opening and closing wings with a fixed camera.
